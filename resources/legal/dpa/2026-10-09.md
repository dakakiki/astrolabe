This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between you, the astrologer who uses AstroLabe for their practice ("you", the **controller**), and **[Business name]**, **[address]**, MB **[…]**, PIB **[…]** ("we", the **processor**). It applies to the personal data of your clients and of the people connected to them that you keep in AstroLabe, and fulfils Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Article 45 of the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (ZZPL). Terms such as *personal data*, *processing*, *controller*, *processor*, *data subject* and *personal data breach* have the meaning given in those laws.

## 1. Subject matter and duration

We process personal data on your behalf to provide AstroLabe. The details — which data, whose, and for what — are in Annex 1. This DPA applies for as long as we process personal data for you under the Terms, and until that data has been deleted as set out in section 10.

## 2. Your instructions

- We process the personal data only on your documented instructions. The Terms, this DPA and what you do with the app's features (entering, changing, exporting or deleting data, sending reminders to yourself) are your instructions. Further instructions can be given by email to **[contact email]**.
- We do not process the data for our own purposes: we do not analyse, sell, rent or use it for advertising or to train models.
- If the law requires us to process the data in another way, we will tell you before we do, unless the law forbids that.
- We will tell you if, in our opinion, an instruction infringes data protection law.

## 3. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the lawfulness of the processing: that you have a legal basis for each client's data, that you inform your clients (Articles 13 and 14 GDPR), that you obtain explicit consent or have another condition where you keep special categories of data (for example health information in notes), and that you keep only what you need. You keep your account secure and decide who in your practice has access.

## 4. Confidentiality

Only people who need it to run the Service may access the personal data, and they are bound to confidentiality. Our operator's administration screens show accounts and figures (numbers of clients, storage used, sign-in times) — never your clients' names, birth data, notes, files or amounts — and every look into them is recorded. Technical access to servers and databases is limited to keeping the Service running, fixing faults and carrying out your instructions.

## 5. Security

We take the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2 and keep them up to date with the state of the art. We may improve them, but never lower the overall level of protection.

## 6. Sub-processors

- You authorise us to use the sub-processors listed in Annex 3.
- We will tell you at least **[30]** days before adding or replacing a sub-processor, by email and in the app. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds and we cannot resolve it, you may end the agreement and export your data before the change applies.
- We bind each sub-processor by contract to data protection obligations at least as strict as those in this DPA, and we remain responsible to you for them.

## 7. Helping you with your clients' requests

AstroLabe gives you the tools to answer your clients' requests yourself: you can view, correct, export and permanently delete a client's data. If a client contacts us directly, we will pass the request to you without delay and not answer it ourselves. Where you need more help — including with a data protection impact assessment or a consultation with a supervisory authority — we will assist as far as is reasonable, considering what we know about the processing.

## 8. Personal data breaches

We will notify you without undue delay, and at the latest within **48 hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, so you can meet your own deadline of 72 hours. The notice describes what happened, the data and people likely affected, the likely consequences, and what we have done or propose to do; information we do not yet have follows as soon as we have it. We will help you inform the authority and your clients where that is needed.

## 9. Where the data is

Your data is stored and backed up on servers in Germany (European Union), operated by our sub-processor. We are established in the Republic of Serbia; where our access to the data from Serbia counts as a transfer to a third country, it is covered by **[the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, module 2 (controller to processor), which form part of this DPA]**. We do not transfer the data anywhere else.

## 10. When the agreement ends

- You can export everything at any time, and at the end (Settings → Your data → Export).
- When you delete your practice, it closes at once and is deleted for good after 30 days, during which you can still cancel and export. A client you delete permanently is removed at once; other deleted items are removed for good 30 days after deletion. Files are removed from the disk together with their records.
- Encrypted backups are kept for 14 days and are not changed; deleted data therefore disappears from them at the latest 14 days after it was deleted.
- We keep nothing else, unless the law requires us to keep it.

## 11. Demonstrating compliance and audits

On request we give you the information needed to show that we meet this DPA. You may audit our compliance, or have it audited by an independent auditor bound to confidentiality, once a year with at least **[30]** days' notice, at your own cost, in a way that does not disclose other customers' data. Where our written information answers your questions, it replaces an on-site audit.

## 12. Liability and order of precedence

Liability is governed by the Terms, without limiting what each party owes to data subjects under Article 82 GDPR. Where this DPA and the Terms differ on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.

## Annex 1 — Details of the processing

**Data subjects.** Your clients and prospective clients; people connected to them whom you record (for example partners, children, parents); and, when teams arrive, the members of your practice.

**Personal data.** As you enter it:

- identity and contact details — name, email address, phone number, notes on how to reach them;
- birth data — date, time and place of birth (with coordinates and time zone), how accurate the time is;
- calculated charts, transits and comparisons;
- consultations — dates, type, your notes and summaries;
- notes and files you upload (documents, images, recordings); location and camera details are removed from images on upload;
- appointments, tasks and reminders;
- payments you received from clients — amount, currency, date, method, reference;
- links between people and tags.

**Special categories.** Notes and files may contain information about health, religious or philosophical beliefs, sex life or sexual orientation, if you write it there. AstroLabe does not ask for it.

**Nature and purpose.** Storing, organising, displaying, calculating, searching, exporting, backing up and deleting the data, and sending you reminders, so that you can run your astrology practice; and keeping the Service secure and working.

**Duration.** For the term of the Terms, then as in section 10.

## Annex 2 — Technical and organisational measures

- **Encryption:** connections only over HTTPS (TLS); backups encrypted (XChaCha20-Poly1305) with a key kept apart from the server; two-factor secrets encrypted in the database.
- **Access to accounts:** passwords stored only as strong one-way hashes and checked against known leaked passwords; optional two-factor sign-in with recovery codes; limits on sign-in attempts and on requests; sessions that end, and can be ended on other devices.
- **Separation:** every practice's data is kept apart in the database, and every request is checked against the practice it belongs to; files are stored privately and downloaded only by authorised members.
- **Accountability:** an audit log of sign-ins, account changes, exports, downloads, deletions and every view or action by the operator's administration (kept 12 months); the operator's administration requires two-factor sign-in and sees metadata only.
- **Data minimisation:** location and camera metadata removed from uploaded images; emails to you carry times and links only, never client names; error reports to the operator carry no message text or client data.
- **Retention:** deleted items removed for good after 30 days, files with them; exports deleted after 7 days; logs rotated after 14 days.
- **Availability:** daily encrypted backups (14 kept) with regular test restores; monitoring of the application, the queue and the backups with alerts to the operator.
- **Servers:** in a data centre in Germany with physical access control (by the sub-processor); firewall allowing only web traffic and administrative access by key; security updates applied automatically; software dependencies checked for known vulnerabilities on every change.
- **Application security:** content security policy and other protective headers; uploaded files checked by their content; no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers in the app.
