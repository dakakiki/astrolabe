## 3. Cookies and storage in your browser

AstroLabe uses only what it needs to work:

- a **session cookie** that keeps you signed in while you use the app;
- a **security cookie** (XSRF-TOKEN) that protects your requests from forgery;
- a **"remember me" cookie**, only if you tick "Keep me signed in";
- your choice of **light or dark theme**, kept in your browser's local storage.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and the app loads no scripts, fonts or images from other websites. That is why AstroLabe does not ask for cookie consent.

## 7. How we protect it

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored as strong hashes and checked against known leaks, optional two-factor sign-in, limits on attempts, every practice's data kept apart, an audit log, encrypted daily backups, servers in a German data centre with security updates. Our operator's administration screens show accounts and figures, never your clients' data. Details are in Annex 2 of the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Much of this you can do yourself: change your details in Settings, export your practice in Settings → Your data, and delete your practice and account there. For anything else write to **[contact email]**; we answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you may complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia (*Poverenik*, www.poverenik.rs), or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## 9. Children

AstroLabe is for professionals aged 18 or over. Accounts are not meant for children.
